
# Global E-Commerce Sales – Business Insights

## Insight 1: Revenue by Product Category
Furniture generated the highest revenue of 253,143.86,
followed by Technology with 139,215.10.

This indicates that Furniture is the leading sales
category and may deserve priority in sales strategies.

## Insight 2: Revenue by Country
Mexico recorded the highest revenue of 47,217.30,
followed by Canada (45,326.55) and the United States
(41,332.53).

This suggests that Mexico is an important market
for the business.

## Insight 3: Monthly Sales Performance
June 2025 generated the highest monthly revenue
of 18,068.09, followed by October 2023 with 17,794.60.

These results identify the strongest individual months,
although more analysis is needed to establish seasonality.

## Insight 4: Top-Performing Products
Standing Desk Converter generated the highest revenue
of 46,614.22, followed by Ergonomic Office Chair
(42,504.04) and Corner L-Shaped Desk (41,070.48).

These are the top three products by revenue.

## Insight 5: Profit by Product Category
Furniture achieved the highest total profit of
80,051.66, followed by Technology with 48,194.59.

This shows that Furniture leads in both total revenue
and total profit.

## Data Source
Results generated from the cleaned Global E-Commerce
Sales practice dataset using Python, Pandas, and SQLite.
